A Night 2 Remember

Sip, Sing & Celebrate with a Purpose

Some nights you remember forever. After losing my dad, I learned I wasn’t alone in my grief, so I started a foundation. This is its first benefit event, celebrating the people we can’t forget, with the songs that got us through, and every ticket gives back.

Monday, October 26 · 7:30 PM to Midnight
The Club Lagos, DTLA

The Why

In January I lost my father. It was my third significant loss in five years, and it showed me I was not the only one carrying something like it. I started Reel Brothers Foundation to walk with people through grief, and A Night 2 Remember is its first benefit event.

$10 from every ticket goes to two foundations, $5 each: Reel Brothers Foundation (national) and My Friend’s House (local, Los Angeles).

Read more

The full story

My father, Michael A. Cummings II, passed in January 2026. Reel Brothers was his idea before it was mine. It came to him after we lost our cousin, who was also his fishing partner, and he never got to build it. I wrote it down in March and built it.

Grief does not end when the funeral does. The foundation exists for the months after, when everyone else has gone back to normal and you are learning a new one. A Night 2 Remember is the first event we are throwing, and it is meant to feel like a celebration, because that is what the people we lost would have wanted.

Where the money goes

Reel Brothers Foundation is a faith based private operating foundation that provides programming and resources to people navigating grief related to the loss of a significant loved one, with a particular focus on young adults. It works nationally.

My Friend’s House empowers individuals and families facing food insecurity and homelessness to achieve self-sufficiency through essential resources, compassionate support, and transformative programs. It works here in Los Angeles.

$10 from every ticket is split evenly between them, $5 each, whatever the turnout.

The Night

Two Parts, One Room

It all happens in one room at The Club Lagos. Come for one part, or stay for both.

7:30 to 9:30

Cocktail Hour + Guided Networking

A welcome drink comes with every ticket and music while the room fills. At 8:30 a host takes the room through a guided hour. Not mingling: real conversation about where people are going next and what they need to get there. You leave knowing someone’s story, and with one thing you can actually do for them.

9:30 to Midnight

DJ + Karaoke Competition

The story, then the champagne toast, then the mic opens. DJ Lefty runs the night and the karaoke competition runs in rounds between his sets, with the crowd voting. Sing one for someone.

What time should I get there?

Doors open at 7:30. The cocktail hour and guided networking run until 9:30, and the DJ and the karaoke competition take the room from 9:30 to midnight.

What is included with my ticket?

Entry, a welcome drink and both parts of the night. The welcome drink includes a non-alcoholic choice. Legacy adds the champagne toast at 9:45 and a flute to keep.

Can I sing?

Yes. Singer registration is free and separate from your ticket. Register when you check out and you are in the lineup when you arrive.

What should I wear?

LA Chic / Cocktail Attire. Dress like you are going out, not like you are going to a gala.
