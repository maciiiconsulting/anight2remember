The Night

One Evening, Two Halves

Monday, October 26, 2026, during NMSDC National Conference Week, at The Club Lagos in Downtown Los Angeles. Come for the first half, stay for the second, or take the whole night.

7:30 to 9:30

The Room

Doors at 7:30 with light food. An intimate room of corporate leaders, certified MBEs and creators, with introductions made rather than left to chance. At 8:30 we tell you why this night exists, and a live performance closes the half.

9:30 to Midnight

The Party

The lights come down and DJ Lefty takes over. The karaoke competition runs in rounds between his sets. Pre-registered singers and live volunteers take the mic, the crowd votes, and the winner takes a special prize before the night closes. Register to sing at no cost and you are in the lineup when you arrive.

LA Chic / Cocktail Attire · 21 and over · #AN2RLA

Why This Night

In January I lost my father. It was my third significant loss in five years, and it showed me I was not the only one carrying something like it. So I started Reel Brothers Foundation, to walk with people through grief.

My Friend’s House has been feeding and clothing people in Downtown Los Angeles for years, in the same neighborhood we are gathering in.

This night is how we fund both, and it is how we celebrate. Come have fun, make a memory, and have a night to remember.

Michael A. Cummings IIIFounder and Board President, Reel Brothers Foundation

A Party for a Purpose

Who This Night Supports

$10 from every ticket goes to the two foundations, $5 each: Reel Brothers Foundation and My Friend’s House.

The Reel Brothers Foundation is a faith based private operating foundation that provides programming and resources to people navigating grief related to the loss of a significant loved one, with a particular focus on young adults.

reelbrothersfoundation.org

Empowering individuals and families facing food insecurity and homelessness to achieve self-sufficiency through essential resources, compassionate support, and transformative programs.
