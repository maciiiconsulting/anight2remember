The Night

A Night 2 Remember brings a karaoke competition to Downtown Los Angeles. Pre-registered singers and live volunteers take the mic, the crowd votes, and the winner takes a bottle. Live music and DJ sets from DJ Lefty run through the night.

Monday, October 26, 2026, during NMSDC National Conference Week, at The Club Lagos in Downtown Los Angeles.

Attire is black tie optional. 21 and over.

Register to sing at no cost and you are in the lineup when you arrive.
